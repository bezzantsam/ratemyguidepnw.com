## Tribal scope

The tribe's fishing page says a Nevada fishing license is not required on Pyramid Lake. A tribal fishing permit is required. A boat needs a current registration from its home state. The department's motor regulation, file date 2025-12-18, amends NAC 488.470 so that only vessels without motors are permitted on the Truckee River from the California line to the point where the river enters the Pyramid Lake Indian Reservation. That sentence is about the river. It does not regulate motors on Pyramid Lake and it is not a guide roster.

https://pyramidlake.us/fishing

https://www.ndow.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/CGR-528-R053-25-Motor-Restrictions-and-Revisions-Related-to-Fisheries-Leg-Commission-Approved-December-2025.pdf

The regulations brochure, effective 2025-10-01 and filed 2025-11-21, is the rule text read for this check. Section 21 says every person who provides fishing guide services inside the reservation, including a fishing boat charter, and whether or not that person is a tribal member, must complete a tribal application and receive Tribal Council approval. The annual fee is $7,500 for a fishing guide and $7,500 for a boat charter. Applications are accepted from June 1 through July 31. The council acts at its first Friday meeting in August. The permit runs from October 1 through September 30. One person is allowed on a permit. The permit is not transferable, and a guide cannot operate under another person's permit. The application must include a liability-insurance certificate, and the applicant pays for a background check. The appearance-bond schedule prints 5000 for operating fishing guide services without tribal approval. The approved-list note says unauthorized guides can face a fine up to $5,000. Section 24 sets a civil fine of not less than $50 and not more than $5,000 for each offense. Those three fine sentences were left as printed.

Angler fees in that brochure are separate from the guide fee: adult one day $27 and three days $70; youth 12 to 17 one day $15 and three days $39; second rod one day $27 and three days $70. The permits page repeats those prices and says there are no seasonal permits. A file dated 2026-09-21 is a working-group proposal. It would close all fishing from July 1 through September 30 and would add a $750 seasonal angler permit sold only from September 8 through September 30. The fishing page and the permits page still show the brochure season and the "no seasonal permits" line. The proposal was not used as the rule in force.

## Unresolved

The proposal of 2026-09-21 and the brochure effective 2025-10-01 still disagree on the summer closure and on a seasonal angler permit. The expired application requires a business license that section 21 does not restate. No open public ramp is named after the Popcorn Beach closure. None of the five pages names a hull. The most useful next primary source is the tribe's business-license ordinance, because that is the permission the expired application adds and the brochure in force does not restate.
