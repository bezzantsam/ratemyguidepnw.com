# Nevada fishing-source inventory

Checked 2026-11-25. This note records roster counts, permission terms, and the shape of a Lake Mead booking sample. Candidate rows stay out of the repository.

## Two credentials

The Nevada Department of Wildlife master-guide license is a person credential. The department's statute page describes a guide as someone who, for compensation, assists another person in hunting wild mammals or wild birds and in fishing, including transport of the person or equipment. The published list covers those activities statewide. It is not a boat list and it is not a Lake Mead list.

Guided fishing inside Lake Mead National Recreation Area also needs a National Park Service commercial use authorization for a fishing charter vessel. That authorization is a business permission. Park terms require a Nevada and/or Arizona master guide license, and a Coast Guard license when a vessel is used. The commercial service has to start and end outside the park. Renting a marina slip does not allow clients to board from that slip. A booking page, a guide license, and a hull are different records. A brand or a captain name was not treated as hull identity.

Testing for new master guides runs January 1 through May 31 by appointment in Elko, Reno, and Las Vegas. Subguide applications are open year-round.

## Lake Mead commercial use

The commercial-use explainer was last updated 2025-09-02:

https://www.nps.gov/lake/getinvolved/cua.htm

The directory page was last updated 2026-10-04. Its table is a CSV, with columns for service type, business name, and business phone:

https://www.nps.gov/lake/getinvolved/cua-directory.htm

The CSV has 288 lines under the header. 178 lines are empty. 110 lines name a business. 108 business names are unique. The service type `Fishing Charter Vessel` appears on 7 lines. Phone numbers were not retained.

An older workbook linked from the explainer was last saved 2025-09-02 and has 109 named rows:

https://www.nps.gov/lake/getinvolved/upload/20250902-Active-CUAs-by-Service-Type.xls

Fishing guide labels in that workbook use four spellings and total 8. Those rows were not matched to the later 7. The two files stay separate denominators.

Fishing-charter conditions, read 2026-11-25:

https://cua.nps.gov/opportunities/by-park/Terms-Conditions/?id=0a866328-a1a6-ef11-8a6a-001dd804edd8

The service must start and end outside the park. Advertising, solicitation, and collecting fees inside the park are outside the authorization, including phone or internet sales while inside the park. The management fee is $5 per client, reported annually. A Nevada and/or Arizona master guide license is required. A vessel requires a Coast Guard operator license for uninspected passenger vessels when there are 6 or fewer passengers, or a Master license when there are 6 or more. The stated minimum liability insurance is $500,000. The application fee is $350, with a $100 reduction for another authorization at the same park in the same season. These are operating conditions. They do not license reuse of operator websites.

NPS website terms, last updated 2025-03-05:

https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/disclaimer.htm

Material created by the National Park Service is generally public domain unless marked otherwise. A commercial republication has to carry a notice that no protection is claimed in original U.S. Government works. Third-party material is not covered. The Arrowhead mark is restricted. A business name on a park page is not an endorsement.

## Booking sample

The sample frame is the 7 `Fishing Charter Vessel` rows. Five had a first-party page with a booking path. Two did not, and no observation was created for them. Reseller pages were not used as proof of where a boat leaves the dock.

Sampled rows equal supported rows plus held rows. The one independent match is a business name that appears both on the October directory and, with fishing status STATEWIDE, on the June master-guide list. That match does not identify a hull or a launch ramp.

All five rows are held:

- No page published a hull identification number, a state registration number, or a vessel name that could be joined to another record.
- No page named a Nevada launch ramp.
- One page tells customers to meet at a gas station beside a Boulder City lodge, then go to an unnamed Lake Mead boat ramp. The lodge's own location page places the property in Boulder City, Nevada, and inside the recreation area: https://hooverdamlodge.com/location/
- Other pages offer Lake Mead together with Lake Mohave or Willow Beach, or they give a Nevada business city that is not a departure.
- Two of the five sites say all rights reserved. Three publish no reuse terms. Silence was recorded as unknown, not as permission.

## Unresolved

The highest-priority permission question is whether the department allows commercial reuse of the master-guide roster, or of the name and business fields only. The posted file contains mailing addresses and phone numbers, and the privacy policy does not answer the question.

The most useful next primary source for embarkation is a park or marina page that names the open Nevada ramps and the courtesy-dock pickup rule. The conditions page checked here does not name the ramps.

Also unresolved: whether the September 2025 and October 2026 fishing authorizations are the same businesses, and whether any public vessel extract exists without home addresses. NRS 239 was cited by the department and was not opened in this pass.
