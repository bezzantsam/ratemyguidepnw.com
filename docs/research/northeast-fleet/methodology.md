# Methodology

1. Identified primary landings via municipal harbor pages, operator vessel pages, and recent party-boat directories.
2. Opened operator sites and official rosters for vessel name, length/description, operator, booking desk (phone or form, no personal emails copied), captain (when named), departure dock/address.
3. Cross-checked independent sources (second operator page or booking platform with matching vessel identity) for each candidate.
4. Recorded direct URL and date checked (2026-11-25).
5. Checked each source for terms of use, attribution, and bulk commercial reuse. None of the private operator sites explicitly permitted commercial directory reuse or bulk download. Government/municipal pages treated as public facts.
6. Identity resolution: look for renamed boats, replacement hulls, or near-identical lengths/operators that a careless importer would merge. Reviews stay with physical hull.
7. Unresolved reuse rights kept out of public candidate records. Synthetic test cases added only for validation of merge logic.
8. No personal contact data, photos, or copied marketing prose in public files.
