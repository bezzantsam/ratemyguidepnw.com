# West Virginia inventory: outfitter licenses and New River Gorge fishing

Checked 2026-11-25. This note records the general outfitter and guide credential, the separate whitewater license, the public company directory, commercial-reuse terms, and aggregate sample counts. Candidate rows stay in the private packet. No personal names, telephones, email addresses, or street lines are in this file.

## Method

The starting point was the WVDNR general outfitter and guide license page. From that page the inventory opened the 2019 application packet, the Series 11 PDF the page still links, the agency copies of §§20-2-23 through 20-2-26, §20-7-12b, and §20-7-16, the 2009 boating rule, the 2019 whitewater rule PDF, and the privacy statement. The Secretary of State indexes for 58 CSR 11 and 58 CSR 12 were opened. The active Series 11 text was read from the Word file because the final PDF is a scanned image. The live code site at code.wvlegislature.gov did not connect, so the code text used here is the agency PDF set created November 8, 2019.

The whitewater how-to page, modified June 9, 2025, was read as a second license product. The whitewater portal's active company directory was counted across its pager. The New River Gorge National Park and Preserve fishing page and the National Park Service disclaimer were opened. Five bookable New River observations were read from first-party operator pages and held. A sixth named site, printed on the portal for New River Dories Guide Service, did not resolve. Fewer than a full pass of the 42 companies was kept because five observations were already in hand and the remaining rows have no boat column to sample. No one was contacted. Tourism roundups were not used as rosters.

A row is supported only when two publishers agree on the same physical hull. A length, a captain role, or a brand, without a second publisher and without a registration or documentation number, stays held. A business-name match on the portal was not counted as a hull match. Sampled rows equal supported plus held on every coverage row. Residence or mailing county is not a departure. Rental length classes are one operation unless a page identifies one physical boat. One boat stated for several rivers is one hull.

## Two licenses, and neither is a hull

The $10 credential is a person or business license for hunting, fishing, and other expeditions:

- Agency-posted §20-2-23 defines an outfitter to include a person who, for profit, provides boats or guide services for fishing or other taking expeditions. An occasional favor rental is outside that definition.
- The applicant elects an outfitter or guide designation. §20-2-24 requires a financially responsible United States citizen who possesses the equipment that is advertised. §20-2-25 collects name, address, property to be used, area to be served, citizenship, and age, with three resident real-property owners of the county as signers.
- §20-2-26, in the November 8, 2019 agency PDF, sets a $10 fee for the calendar year on the license and a $1,000 surety bond for the life of the license. The director may revoke the license.
- Active 58 CSR 11 is effective April 19, 2024, and sunsets August 1, 2029. Licenses expire December 31 of the year of issue. Liability minimums in that text are $300,000 bodily injury per occurrence and $25,000 property damage, with proof due by February 15. First aid is required for the person conducting the service. Boats must be kept in safe operable condition.
- The Secretary of State index cites §§20-1-7(30) and 20-2-23. The Word file cites §§20-1-7(31) and 20-2-23. Both lines are recorded. The discrepancy was not resolved because the legislature host did not connect.
- The license page still links a Series 11 PDF filed and effective April 26, 2018, which sunsets April 26, 2023. That copy is historical.
- Form DNR-LE-317, dated 11.06.2019, is a 49-page packet created and modified November 6, 2019. Section 6 is an item-and-quantity list of property used in the service. It is not a hull-identification number. A Social Security number was not on the form pages that were read.
- A motorboat guide born on or after December 31, 1986 needs the boater-education certificate in §20-7-12b unless the guide holds a federal commercial operator license. That certificate is not a guide roster.
- A boat livery under §20-7-16 keeps a renter record that includes a vessel identification number. Series 25, effective July 1, 2009, adds a livery safety orientation. A livery is not the outfitter license. No rental log was collected.
- The customer's West Virginia fishing license is a separate requirement. The park fishing page states it, and one operator page tells the angler to obtain it before arrival.

The Whitewater Outfitter and Guide License is a different product. The how-to page was published June 11, 2021, and modified June 9, 2025. It states a $250 non-refundable application with a letter of intent, a March 15 filing for a license effective January 1 of the next calendar year, and a commission deadline printed as September 31. After approval the annual fee is $500 per license. Before operation the licensee submits a $1,000 bond, commercial general liability insurance, and an equipment and location inspection that includes CPR and first aid. The insurance-and-bond PDF linked from that page was not opened.

The $10 license page describes a different whitewater duty: user fees of $0.70 on the Gauley and $0.35 on other rivers, with a Law Enforcement reporting login. Active Series 11 states study fees of $0.35 per customer on the Cheat, New, Shenandoah, and Tygart study zones and $0.70 on the Gauley, due the 15th of the next month. Active Series 12, effective April 19, 2024 under §20-2-23a, was indexed and not read. The Series 12 PDF the license page still links was filed March 27, 2019, effective March 28, 2019, and sunset March 28, 2024. Its New River zone sentences were not used as current law.

## What the portal is

https://whitewater.wvdnr.gov/OutfittersAndGuides?Inactive=False is a public company directory. Pages 1 through 3 contain 20, 20, and 2 rows. Page 4 returns and has no rows. The count is 42 active companies. Columns are company, address, county, phone, and website. There is no boat, license number, or waterbody, and no printed revision date. Nine rows have no website. The directory is not linked from the $10 license page, and the page does not say these 42 rows are every general outfitter in the state. Telephones and street lines were not copied. County and mailing city are not departures. The check date is November 25, 2026. The source date of the list is unprinted.

The privacy statement says that, except as required by law, the division does not share information gathered on the website, including email addresses, with any private or commercial organization. The site footer is copyright 2026. That sentence is not a directory grant. The whitewater portal page itself prints no terms, so reuse of the 42-row list is unknown. The Secretary of State footer is copyright 2026 and is a citation of the rule index, not a license to republish the directory.

National Park Service material on nps.gov is generally a U.S. government work under 17 U.S.C. §§ 101 and 105 unless indicated otherwise. A citation is appreciated. Commercial republication must include a notice that no protection is claimed in original U.S. government works, under 17 U.S.C. § 403. The arrowhead trademark is excluded. That disclaimer does not license a state roster. The New River Gorge fishing page was last updated September 30, 2024. It names public access from Hinton through Fayette Station and requires a West Virginia fishing license in the park. It is not a guide roster.

## Open question

The blocking permission question is whether a New River fishing float needs the $500 whitewater license or only the $10 general license plus the per-user study fee. The most useful next primary source is the active Series 12 Word file, Secretary of State document 57123. The private packet also lists the legislature code page that did not connect, the unopened insurance-and-bond PDF, the whitewater licenses-and-forms page whose body was not read, and the contact page, which was not opened and was not used to file a request. A further question is whether the 2016 G3 is the Upper New float boat or a different hull, and whether any first-party page replaces the dories host that did not resolve.
