## The credential is a vessel license

R.I. Gen. Laws § 20-2-27.1 and the active marine-fisheries rule require a Rhode Island party and charter vessel license for party and charter boats that carry recreational passengers to take, or to attempt to take, marine fish on Rhode Island navigable and coastal waters. The license is held for the vessel. It is not a person guide roster.

The active rule is 250-RICR-90-00-2, Commercial Marine Fishing Licenses, Landing Permits, and Party and Charter Licenses, effective 2026-01-01 through 2027-01-01. The rule page says a later amendment becomes effective on 2027-01-01. That later text was not used. Section 2.9 matches the statute on these points:

- The owner applies and supplies a current Coast Guard license to carry passengers for hire.
- The owner supplies a coastwise Certificate of Documentation, or a state registration if the vessel is under five net tons.
- The operator and crew must be enrolled in a random drug-testing program under 46 C.F.R. § 16.101 and following.
- The owner certifies state and federal safety compliance.
- The fee is $100 for a resident and $300 for a nonresident.
- The license expires on December 31.
- Trips are logged in SAFIS before the trip ends and submitted within 48 hours. A year with no party or charter trips still needs a negative report. Missing reports can bring suspension, revocation, or a bar on renewal.

## Port of Galilee

Port rules are 250-RICR-50-00-1, Port Operations Regulations and Berthing Management System, active and effective 2025-10-07. The Port of Galilee is the improved and unimproved area of Narragansett in the west passage. The Secretary of State HTML was read. The linked official PDF was not opened as a separate file.

The rule separates two recreational for-hire types. A charter boat is under 100 gross tons and meets Coast Guard requirements to carry six or fewer passengers for hire for recreational fishing. A headboat or party boat holds a Coast Guard Certificate of Inspection to carry more than six passengers for hire for recreational fishing.

Charter berth applicants must be engaged as charter boats that year, hold the federal passengers-for-hire licenses and the state charter licenses, and show coastwise documentation if over five tons or transaction records of passengers for hire on at least 20 days in the previous year. Headboat applicants need a Coast Guard license for more than six passengers, a current certificate of inspection, the state party and charter requirements, coastwise documentation, and the same 20-day history. A vessel needs Coast Guard documentation or a Rhode Island certificate of numbers, plus a commercial fishing declaration or a Rhode Island party and charter license, to take or keep a berth.

The annual berth rate is $40 per foot. Transient vessels are $50 per day, plus $1 per foot per day over 50 feet. New sport-fishing berths are not issued. The division assigns berths and keeps a waiting list. No berth-assignment list was published in the rule. The berth population is null. Sampled, supported, and held rows for that list are 0. Parking Area E is for commercial fishermen and for charter and headboat crews who hold berths, and for their customers. Newport state pier berths are outside this phase.
