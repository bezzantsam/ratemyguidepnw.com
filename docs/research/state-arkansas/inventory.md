# Arkansas fishing-directory inventory

Checked 2026-11-25. Phase `arkansas-inventory`. The water in this pass is the White River tailwater near Cotter. This note records the credential, the reuse terms that were actually opened, and the aggregate result of a four-row sample. People, telephone numbers, email addresses, street addresses, and postal boxes stay outside this repository.

No one was contacted. This file is not a listing and not a publishing decision.

## What is licensed

The starting page is the AGFC fishing-license fee table. An angler who is 16 or older needs a fishing license to take aquatic wildlife, with an exception for a licensed put-and-take pay lake. A trout permit is required to fish designated tailwaters, including the tailwater below Bull Shoals, or to keep trout from any Arkansas water. On that table the resident fisheries conservation license is $10.50, the nonresident annual fishing license is $60, the resident trout permit is $10, and the nonresident trout permit is $20.

The fishing-guide credential is a person license. Code 03.14, headed as of July 1, 2025, requires a resident who guides fishing for pay to carry a resident fishing license, a Resident Guide License/Fishing (GLF) or a Resident Combination Guide License (GLC), and proof of at least $100,000 general liability insurance for the guide's business. The hunting half of that section also requires a Resident Special Guide License when the hunt is on a wildlife management area. The penalty is Class 2. A few words in the PDF are spaced oddly. The three fishing requirements are readable.

Code 03.15, headed as of January 3, 2025, requires a nonresident fishing guide to carry a nonresident fishing license, a Nonresident Guide License/Fishing, and the same $100,000 insurance. That section also says a nonresident may not exercise Resident Special Guide License rights. The penalty is Class 3. Both sections were adopted under Amendment 35 to the Arkansas Constitution. Neither section licenses a boat.

The fee table lists GLF at $250, GLC at $350, and the nonresident fishing guide license (GNF) at $500. GLF and GNF do not include fishing privileges. The commercial-licenses page says GLC expires June 30. The GLF and GNF rows on the pages opened do not state an expiration. The same commercial page lists a Resident Commercial Fish Permit at $300 for commercial tackle, valid through December 31 and sold at headquarters. That permit is a different credential from the guide license.

The purchase portal linked from the fee page is an account and cart shell. A control labeled Search for Existing Customer assigns an existing customer account to a license. The shell that loaded did not present a public guide roster, a bulk file, or a field dictionary. The statewide count of fishing-guide licenses is unknown.

## Guide ARK

Guide ARK is a directory of the Arkansas Office of Outdoor Recreation, featured on Arkansas.com. The about page says every listed guide is licensed and insured, and that listing a business is free and voluntary. Page metadata on that about page carries a modified date of March 13, 2026. A profile includes business and contact information. Those contact details were not copied.

The find-a-guide page says listings are informational and are not an endorsement. It tells the reader to confirm licensure and insurance with the guide, and it notes extra permits for U.S. Forest Service land, the Buffalo National River, wildlife management areas, and private property.

The directory page, opened without filters, says it is showing 1–15 of 29. The filters include Fishing Guides and White River. Those filters were not applied. The first visible card describes crappie fishing in Central Arkansas, so the 29 count mixes activities and waters. The White River fishing subset is unknown. This directory is a voluntary tourism list. It is not an extract of the AGFC license roll.

An Office of Outdoor Recreation data story describes hunting and fishing license purchases. Its footnote defines the 2024 season as July 1, 2023 through June 30, 2024. The story does not publish a fishing-guide holder count.

Ember Shoals is supported as a booking desk. Cotter Elite Anglers is held. One operator site dates the service to 2024 on the White and Norfork. A 2026 rates image was not transcribed. The booking control is a telephone link. No second publisher, no terms page, and no hull were found. Absence from the AGFC pages is not a licensure finding, because those pages publish no holder list.

## Where to start

Read Code 03.14 and Code 03.15, then the fee table. Until AGFC publishes a holder list, a Cotter guide is an operator claim plus the person license the code requires the guide to carry. Guide ARK can confirm a voluntary tourism listing after the Fishing Guides and White River filters are applied. Add a hull only when a public record names that hull.
