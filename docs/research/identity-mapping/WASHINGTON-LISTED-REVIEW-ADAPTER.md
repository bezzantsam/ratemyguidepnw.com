## Database contract

The adapter runs after the final launch body, Oregon split, prospective pilot, and listed identity proof in the disposable runner. It neither creates nor enables a binding on application. The owner-only carrier procedure checks the exact approved source ID, listing digest and intake packet digest against the already prepared and disabled ledger. It creates one neutral draft using the ledger's fresh UUID and an opaque internal slug. A trigger prevents publishing, repointing, deleting or adding license fields to that carrier. The internal row is absent from anonymous guide discovery and carries no person or business name. It has no legacy guide map and no claims lookup.

The versioned review resolver is confined to the review path. The general guide resolver remains published-guide-only and keeps its previous Oregon behavior. Existing write, publication, revision, reporting, ratings and authenticated actor logic apply. A mere held neighbor, a stale digest or a bare WA ID stays closed. The private WA listed binding cannot confer eligibility while the prospective WA pilot is enabled. An author sees their own receipt under the existing stable source ID, while independent moderators use the existing queues and decisions.

The existing listed-identity disable SQL pauses just this WA binding and serializes with admitted review writes. On pause, the existing profile API closes, the pending WA item leaves the moderator queue and the listed carrier's previously published reviews and ratings are hidden through both the public view and direct public review-table reads. Publication state, scores, audit, mapping, carrier, author receipts and review rows are retained. Oregon public reads and readiness continue. The prospective pilot's pause behavior and claims gate are unchanged.
