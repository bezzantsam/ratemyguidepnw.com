# New Mexico fishing-source inventory

Checked 2026-11-25. This note records roster counts, permission terms, and the shape of a San Juan River quality-water sample below Navajo Dam. Candidate rows stay out of the repository.

## Three credentials

A State Parks fishing-guide concession is permission to guide inside a park. It is not a boat list. The public explainer, read 2026-11-25, says each application includes one guide card and one hang tag. The Navajo Lake concession is $500 and includes one vehicle hang tag. Every other listed park is $300 and includes one vehicle hang tag. The same page states the concession fee as $300 except Navajo Lake State Park at $500. The permit year runs April 1 through March 31. The applicant needs a valid fishing license, a New Mexico tax identification number, and a Secretary of State good-standing letter. Insurance must be at least $1,000,000 each occurrence and $2,000,000 general aggregate, and must name the State, the Energy, Minerals and Natural Resources Department, the State Parks Division, and the property owner as co-insured. Boating knowledge is marked preferred. It is not stated as required. More than one guide may be listed on a card, and only one guide may be on the lake or river at a time. Additional cards can be purchased for simultaneous trips. The page says there is a fee for each additional card and does not state that dollar amount. The outfitter may not conduct business until the guide cards are in hand. A trip-ticket login is sent to the permit holder. That login is not a public roster.

https://www.emnrd.nm.gov/spd/work-with-parks/outfitters/

An angler fishing license is a separate Department of Wildlife product. The concession page requires the guide to hold one. It does not make the angler-license file a guide roster.

A registered hunting outfitter or hunting guide is a third credential. The Department of Wildlife hunting page is the hunting program. It was not used as the San Juan fishing denominator. The active hunting-outfitter download was not opened, so its row count is null.

https://wildlife.dgf.nm.gov/hunting/guide-outfitter-information/

## Quality water and the river boat rule

The 2026-2027 fishing-rules booklet states two San Juan lines that were read. Special trout water runs from Navajo Dam downstream 3.75 miles to the Crusher Hole day-use area, and more than two flies or lures on one line is illegal there. A separate specific-waters line says the San Juan below Navajo Dam has no bag or possession limit for channel catfish or striped bass, and that special trout water allows only two flies per line. The extracts do not assign that reach to Red Chile, Green Chile, or Xmas Chile water, and they do not restate a catch-and-release bag.

https://wildlife.dgf.nm.gov/download/new-mexico-fishing-rules-and-info/?wpdmdl=54215

An older special-trout download contains the same 3.75-mile sentence and the same two-fly sentence. Its file date was not printed in the extract. It was not used as a newer authority than the 2026-2027 booklet.

https://wildlife.dgf.nm.gov/download/special-trout-waters/?wpdmdl=44445

The State Parks fishing page, indexed 2021-06-07, says the first 4 miles below Navajo Dam are special trout water, the first 3.5-mile section is catch-and-release, and 17 miles are managed for rainbow and brown trout. That page is older than the booklet. The two mileage sentences and the catch-and-release sentence were not merged.

https://www.emnrd.nm.gov/spd/activities/fishing/

A September 2005 fisheries management plan says the trout management area runs from the dam downstream 17 miles to the U.S. 64 bridge at Blanco, and that the upper 4 miles are special trout water. That is a historical claim.

https://wildlife.dgf.nm.gov/download/san-juan-river-management-plan/?wpdmdl=44427

The replacement text of 18.17.2 NMAC, posted 2024-07-26, contains section 18.17.2.16 G. Motorized vessels are prohibited on the San Juan River inside Navajo Lake State Park. Non-motorized vessels are authorized from the Texas Hole day-use area downstream and are limited to fishing unless the director authorizes otherwise. A February 2024 proposed-rule packet left the effective date blank and was not used as the in-force text. A motorboat ramp at Pine or at Sims is a lake ramp. It is not the quality-water departure in that river rule.

https://www.emnrd.nm.gov/spd/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/18.17.2-NMAC-replaced-7.26.24.pdf

The 2026-2027 booklet says motorized and sail-powered vessels 10 feet or longer need a title, and motorized vessels of any length must be registered through the Motor Vehicle Division. Unpowered drift boats are outside that sentence. No public hull search was opened. The public hull denominator is null.

The 2023 park map names Texas Hole day use, Crusher Hole day use, Cottonwood campground, Munoz/Lower Flats, Simon Point, the Bureau of Reclamation day-use area, Pumphouse, and the Pine and Sims lake areas. A named area on the map is not a sampled business.

All five sampled rows are held:

- No page published a hull identification number, a state registration number, or a vessel name that could be joined to another record.
- Drift-boat and wade products were described without naming a hull. A model-class boat without a name or number was not supported.
- One site states a drift-boat launch at a named lodge. That is an operator departure claim. It is not the Texas Hole sentence in the river rule, and it is not a hull identifier. A lake-boat product on the same site was not joined to the river boat.
- One site's pages disagree on the float price range. Those ranges were not averaged.
- One site's prices were not present as text. Image captions that use a captain title were not treated as a credential record or as a hull.
- A photograph filename or image description was not treated as the departure for every trip.
- A lower-river product was kept separate from a quality-water product.
- A business place, a lodge, and a lake marina were not treated as the quality-water departure.
- Operator pages either show a copyright year with no license sentence, or they are silent. None of the five pages read reserved all rights in a license sentence. Silence is unknown, not permission.
- The directory owner field was not copied and was not used to join a person to a boat.
