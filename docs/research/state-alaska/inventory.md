# Alaska fishing-directory inventory

Checked 2026-11-25. Phase `alaska-inventory`. The landing in this pass is the Homer small-boat harbor. This note is the public method: which credentials exist, what each publisher allows a commercial directory to do, and the aggregate result of a five-row Homer vessel sample. Business names, guide names, hull names, phone numbers, email addresses, and street addresses stay outside this repository.

No one was contacted. This file is not a listing and not a publishing decision.

## What is registered

The saved lead is the sport-fish guide and business registration page. It is an application, not a lookup. Alaska registers three different things before guided sport fishing for compensation:

- A business registers each year and receives a business registration card. The application asks for the owner's name and mailing contacts, and for a current State of Alaska business license. Those contact fields were not copied.
- A guide registers each year and receives a guide registration card. The guide must also hold a current Alaska sport fishing license. An assistant or deckhand working on the same vessel under a registered guide is exempt from guide registration.
- A vessel registers each year with the Department of Fish and Game. Registration is separate from a Division of Motor Vehicles boat number. The department issues two year stickers and, when needed, two decals. Saltwater guiding also uses the department's electronic logbook, issued to the registered business. Freshwater guiding still requires business, guide, and vessel registration. Freshwater trip reporting ended in 2019.

AS 16.05.395, read from the Legislature's Title 16 PDF on this date, says a person may not use a vessel to provide sport fishing services unless the vessel is registered with the department and proof of registration is attached and clearly visible. The department registers those vessels and provides the proof without charge.

The department's vessel page says a vessel used for sport-fish guided activities for more than 90 days needs a current Alaska boat number before department registration, unless the vessel has a unique U.S. Coast Guard documentation number. The only exemption named on that page is a single-person flotation tube. The Cornell Law School compilation of 5 AAC 75.077, last amended June 25, 2023, Register 246, requires the motor-vehicle boat number or the Coast Guard documentation number for each vessel at registration. That compilation text does not contain a 90-day threshold. Float tubes are the exemption there. Cornell is a quarterly compilation, not the Lieutenant Governor's filed register. The Legislature's code browser did not return the section text from the fragment link. The two statements are both recorded. The official filed section is still the controlling text to read next.

A Kenai River guide and vessel also register with the Department of Natural Resources. That is a different water and a different decal. It was not sampled.

The 2026 industry letter is a PDF created April 3, 2026. It reminds businesses and guides who were registered in 2025, or who registered for the first time in 2026, that businesses, guides, and vessels in salt water and fresh water register for 2026, and that registration is free. It is a letter to those recipients. It is not a directory and it has no count.

The program hub, the application page, the requirements page, the vessel page, and the electronic-logbook page were opened. None of them links to a public roster, a download, or a field dictionary of registered businesses, guides, or vessels. Three guessed paths under the same section, `lookup`, `directory`, and `list`, returned the department's website-error page on this date. `lookup` redirected to the page-not-found endpoint.

The commercial permit-holder page is a current-year list of processors, buyers, transporters, catcher-sellers, and direct marketers. It is a commercial-fishing credential. It was not used as a sport-fish guide roster and it was not counted.

The privacy statement cites 2 AAC 96.100 through 2 AAC 96.900 and says information provided to obtain a sport fish guide license is public information, except a social security number, a driver's license number, financial information such as a credit card number, and certain harvest data. A person can mark a preference not to have that information shared. That sentence describes public-records status. It does not publish a file, and it does not grant republication.

## Homer harbor

The City of Homer reserved-moorage page says the harbor has 920 reserved stalls, leased from October 1 through September 30. A stall on the wait list is tied to the person or entity that signed up, and the page says the stall is not sold with the boat. The reserved-stall permit, terms revised 2025, is a revocable permit for the vessel named on that permit. The permit says it applies to that vessel and is not transferable to another owner or another vessel. A new vessel needs a new permit. The blank form asks for the vessel name, length, beam, and draft, a use box that includes charter, and at least one of an Alaska motor-vehicle number, a Fish and Game number, or a Coast Guard documentation number. It also asks for owner and operator contact fields. Those fields were not copied. No completed permit, vessel name, or hull number is posted on the pages opened. The count 920 is a stall count. It is not a charter-hull census.

## Reuse

A current page, a download, and authority to claim a listing are three different things.

| Publisher | Reuse status | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alaska Department of Fish and Game website | not_granted | Copyright page: copying department material onto a non-department page requires permission first. A fair-use download, such as a paper copy for reading, is allowed. Republication is prohibited. Silence after a request is not permission. The department may revoke a license it grants. Frames that display the department site are prohibited. Authority cited on the page: AS 16.05.050(2), AS 16.05.050(8), and AS 44.99.400. The terms page points back to that copyright policy. |
| Sport fish registration pages and the April 3, 2026 letter | not_granted | Same copyright policy. The pages and the letter describe the registration program. They are not a bulk file and they are not a directory license. |
| Privacy statement | not_granted | Public-records status of license application data is not a license to republish it. Specified sensitive fields are excluded. A non-sharing preference can be honored. |
| Alaska Legislature Title 16 PDF | unknown | AS 16.05.395 was read from the compiled statutes PDF. The PDF metadata has no terms grant. The Legislature disclaimer was not opened. |
| Cornell compilation of 5 AAC 75.077 | unknown | The regulation text was read there. That host's terms were not opened. The compilation is not the official register. |
| City of Homer harbor pages | conditional | Website terms, adopted with Resolution 15-026, encourage sharing of information on the city site and retain copyright on design, logos, photographs, and code. The terms are not a contract and can change without notice. They do not grant a commercial directory, and the harbor pages do not publish a vessel roster. |
| Four operator publishers | not_granted (2), unknown (2) | Two fleet pages carry a 2026 copyright line and no directory license. Two pages presented no terms URL. |

The highest-priority permission question is whether the department will give written permission before any business, guide, or vessel registration record is republished, and whether it will release a current file at all. The most useful next primary source is the official text of 5 AAC 75.077, then the department's records process for the registration file. A 404, a stall count, and an operator's own booking page do not authorize a commercial directory to claim a listing.

## Where to start

Read the business, guide, and vessel pages as three credentials. Read AS 16.05.395 and the official text of 5 AAC 75.077 before treating the vessel page's 90-day sentence as the rule. Until a public vessel file exists, a Homer charter hull is an operator claim. Add a hull only when a dated public record prints the Alaska number or the Coast Guard documentation number and that number matches one vessel.
