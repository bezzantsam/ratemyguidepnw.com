# Kentucky inventory: guide licenses, Kentucky Lake, and Lake Barkley

Checked 2026-11-25. This note records the fishing guide and outfitter credentials, the public name list, commercial-reuse terms, and aggregate sample counts. Candidate rows stay in the private packet. Three operator domains are personal or surname names and are omitted from this file.

## Method

The starting point was the KDFWR commercial guide license page. From that page the inventory opened 301 KAR 3:150, the public outfitter PDF, both 2025 fishing applications, the fee table, the department privacy statement, the Commonwealth data policy, the external-link disclaimer, the open-records procedures, and the commercial-purpose certification. The certification is a scanned form and returned no text. A regulation-summary page titled Hunting and Fishing Regulation Summary Guides was opened because the fees page links a guides path. It does not carry the holder PDF.

Kentucky Lake and Lake Barkley were kept apart. Lake Barkley Marina and Kentucky Dam Marina were opened, including the Barkley rental agreement and the Kentucky Dam FAQ. Four bookable fishing observations were read from operator pages: one 21-foot Skeeter stated for both lakes, one 20-foot bass boat stated for Kentucky Lake, one unnamed bowfishing boat stated for both lakes and three rivers, and one brand page that offers both lakes and states no hull. Fewer than five were kept because the next named businesses on the fishing list had no opened first-party page. No one was contacted. Tourism roundups were not used as rosters.

A row is supported only when two publishers agree on the same physical hull. A length, a captain, or a brand, without a second publisher and without a registration or documentation number, stays held. Sampled rows equal supported plus held on every coverage row. A business-name match on the license PDF was counted as a second publisher of a hull only when that PDF also stated the hull. It never did.

## License, holder, and hull

301 KAR 3:150 and the license page describe person credentials:

- A fishing outfitter license authorizes a person to solicit for or provide guiding services, including bowfishing. Tier I is zero fishing guides. Tier II is one or more.
- A fishing guide license authorizes a person to assist that outfitter's clients. The guide is employed by or contracted with the outfitter, and the license is issued only when the outfitter has listed the guide.
- The license year is March 1 through the last day of February. The rule was filed October 14, 2025, and is effective November 6, 2025. The compilation page was generated August 28, 2026. A seven-year expiration is printed as November 6, 2032.
- Resident and nonresident fees on both the requirements page and the fee table are $105.70 and $422.80 for a fishing guide, $264.25 and $1,057.00 for Tier I, and $792.75 and $1,585.50 for Tier II. Hunting guide and hunting outfitter tiers are separate products. A commercial fishing license with 10 gear tags, $158.55 resident and $634.20 nonresident, is a harvest product on the fee table.
- Applicants are 18 or older, with no fish, boat, or wildlife conviction in the previous three years and no felony. The outfitter and the guide hold their own annual fishing license. A fishing helper is a person in the same boat, or alongside on a stream or riverbank, and holds a Kentucky fishing license. The helper is not a hull.
- Guiding from a boat requires boater education or a USCG captain's license or six-pack, which must be presented on the boat. The license page allows the six-pack to stand in for boater education, CPR, and first aid when the water is USCG-regulated. The applications say the same substitution in the OUPV form. Neither application asks for a boat, a lake, or a documentation number. A Social Security number is on the form and was not copied.

Nothing read here says the outfitter license covers the customer's fishing license. One held operator page tells the angler to bring a fishing license.

## What the PDF is

The license page points to a nine-page PDF, created and modified November 4, 2026, footer Revised 11/04/2026. Pages 1 through 4 are marked hunting licensed and contain 95 ZIP rows. Pages 5 through 9 are marked fishing licensed and contain 114 ZIP rows. The columns are name, business name, and a city, state, and ZIP. There is no waterbody and no boat. A residence city is not a departure. The 114 fishing rows are the statewide fishing-credential count on that file. They are not a Kentucky Lake fleet. The 95 hunting rows stay out of the fishing total. The check date of this inventory is November 25, 2026. The source date of the file is November 4, 2026.

The rule requires a monthly fishing report, due by the 15th for the previous month even when no guiding occurred, through the department activity portal. The report fields include the waterbody each day. The portal and the fishing-log PDFs were linked and not opened. They are not a public hull list.

The department privacy statement prohibits the sale of collected information. Contractor use is limited by the agency agreement. The Commonwealth data policy, with a 2022 rights footer, says personal information will not be provided, rented, or sold for marketing, and it is silent on a directory grant. The disclaimer is an external-link notice with the same 2022 footer. Open-records inspection runs to a resident of the Commonwealth. A commercial purpose, defined to include sale, resale, solicitation, rent, or expected profit, requires a certified statement under KRS 61.874(4)(b). Newspaper, broadcast news, and litigation uses are outside that definition. Opening the page did not authorize a commercial directory. The certification PDF, created July 22, 2020, has no text layer. A request was not filed. The Legislative Research Commission footer on the rule reserves all rights as of 2024. The use made of that page is the citation of 301 KAR 3:150.

## Landings

Lake Barkley Marina places itself at Lake Barkley State Resort Park in Cadiz. The page has a ship store, slips, and boat rentals. It does not list a guide. The menu item for terms opens a boat-rental agreement and waiver, which is a lessee contract. The footer is copyright 2024, all rights reserved. A news line on the homepage is dated September 19, 2026. That date is not a hull date.

Kentucky Dam Marina is on Kentucky Lake in Marshall County. Rental classes on the homepage include an Aloha 25 Tritoon, a Double Decker Pontoon, a Barletta Tritoon, a Bayliner 18 fishing boat, paddleboards, and kayaks. Those classes are the rental fleet. They were not sampled as guide hulls. The FAQ says the marina does not sell fishing licenses and does not allow fishing from the dock. Dam construction from 1941 to 1950 is a dam date. No copyright line was on the homepage. The linked privacy policy was not opened, so reuse of that site is unknown.

Two guessed Kentucky State Parks explore URLs returned the parks 404 page. The marina pages are the landings that were read.
