# Illinois fishing-charter inventory

Checked 2026-11-25. Scope is the Illinois passenger-for-hire boat license and Lake Michigan fishing trips that depart from Waukegan. This note is methodology, aggregate counts, primary-source links, and unresolved permission questions. It does not identify operators or people.

## Credential

The assigned lead is the Department of Natural Resources passenger and rental boat license page. It is a license for a business that operates a boat carrying passengers for hire, or that rents boats for hire. It is not a fishing-guide license, and it is not limited to fishing trips.

The compiled Boat Registration and Safety Act, 625 ILCS 45/7-1, source Public Act 104-137 effective January 1, 2026, requires that license from the Department. Renewal on that compiled page is January 1, and the license runs through December 31. The Department is to set the application process by rule. Section 7-2 of the same compilation says the passenger-boat fee and the rental fees shall be set by administrative rule. The compiled fee sentence is garbled and states no dollar amount. Section 7-7 requires the license to be displayed and a tag or disc to be placed on each licensed boat.

The Department webpage, read the same day, still says the license renews on March 1 and that the fee to carry passengers for hire is $50 for each boat. It says the rental business fee is $30 plus a per-boat length fee on the application. The passenger application file, last modified January 11, 2023, repeats $50 per boat, March 1, and a requirement to submit an inspection report and a U.S. Coast Guard operator license for each operator. The rental application file of the same date prints $30, plus $1, $2, or $8 by length, and is a different form. A 2023 information brochure restates the older March 1 dates and those dollar amounts. It is not a compilation of the 2026 act.

Title 17, Part 2080, of the Illinois Administrative Code is the passengers-for-hire rule. The part that was read contains no dollar fee. Section 2080.70, effective July 28, 1999, says the passengers-for-hire license expires on March 1 of the following year, is posted on the vessel, and is issued with one decal. Section 2080.60 requires a Coast Guard license to operate passengers for hire on the navigable waters of the state, and it requires every qualified operator to be listed on the Department license. Section 2080.65, effective June 23, 2021, requires the application to name each operator and to include that person's merchant mariner credential. The only use of the word fishing in the part is stored fishing gear before an inspection. Rental boats are a later pair of sections in the same part. They are not this charter roster.

The passenger application collects an establishment, an owner, operators, and, for each of two boats, length, beam, capacity, a documentation number, an Illinois registration number, and a port. The inspection report for not more than six passengers adds make, model, and hull identification. Those fields are not published. The report for more than six passengers was not opened.

## Public list

No public roster, name lookup, or download of passenger-for-hire licensees was found on the license page or in Part 2080. The issued-license count is unknown.

The Department commercial-license fee table returned 32 license types. None is a passenger-boat license, a rental-boat license, or a fishing-guide license. One row is a waterfowl outfitting permit. That permit was not used. Angler licenses are a different table and were not used. A metadata query on the state open-data portal for this subject returned an unrelated dataset and no license roster. The query was not a census of the portal. The portal terms were not read.

Department website notices disclaim accuracy and say third-party material on the site is not an endorsement. They do not grant or prohibit copying Department text into a commercial directory. The FOIA page says agency records may be requested in writing under 5 ILCS 140, and that some records are exempt. The Act itself was not opened. Watercraft registration is mentioned there as information already available for a fee. That is not this license.

## Waukegan departure list

The Waukegan Harbor and Marina charter page was still online on the check date. Its article modified time is March 19, 2018. The footer copyright is 2020, all rights reserved. No separate terms page was found.

Counted from the table: 10 business rows. Columns are a charter name, a captain, a boat name, a phone, a website, and an email. One row prints two boat names, so the page contains 11 boat-name strings. The columns do not state length, make, registration, documentation, slip, or whether the trip is fishing. A phone count was not used as the row count.

Five of the ten business rows were compared with a current first-party page. Four were supported. One was held. Sampled = supported + held: 5 = 4 + 1.

Each supported row is a current fishing page that names one hull with a length and a make, and the harbor page prints that same boat name. The harbor page does not confirm the measurement. Its 2018 modified time is not a 2026 slip assignment and not a Department license. No registration or documentation number was on those pages. One of the supported rows is the harbor row that prints two boat names. The current page for that business names three hulls and gives them one shared length and make. Only the single name that appears on both pages was supported. The other harbor name was not joined to either of the remaining names. A shared brand is not a hull.

The held row is a current fishing page with a length and a make on a named hull. The harbor row for that business prints a different boat name. The names were not joined. A shared business is not a hull.

Four harbor rows were not opened. One other hostname on the page returned HTTP 520 on two attempts and was left outside the five. The full Waukegan fleet is not these ten rows.

The harbor resources page, modified February 28, 2022, links a hostname it describes as the Waukegan Charter Boat Association. That URL, fetched the same day, is a different publisher and does not list a Waukegan fleet.

## Commercial reuse

A license requirement, a public list, and authority to copy either one into a commercial directory are separate. No public license list was found.

- Department legal notices on the privacy page do not state a directory license. Silence is unknown. They are not a grant to claim a listing.
- The application collects hull and operator fields for the Department. It does not mention a third-party directory.
- The harbor charter page says all rights reserved. No attribution formula and no permission form were found. That notice is not a directory grant.
- Operator pages in the sample either reserve all rights in a copyright line or say nothing about reuse. A copyright line is not a directory license. Silence is unknown.

Nothing checked here authorizes this directory to claim a listing.
