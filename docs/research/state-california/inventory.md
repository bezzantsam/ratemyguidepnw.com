# California fishing-directory inventory

Checked 2026-11-25. Phase `california-inventory`. The landing in this pass is the Sonoma County Sport Fishing Center at Bodega Bay, outside San Diego. This note is the public method: which credentials exist, what each publisher allows a commercial directory to do, and the aggregate result of a five-row vessel sample. Business names, guide names, hull names, phone numbers, email addresses, and street addresses stay outside this repository.

No one was contacted. This file is not a listing and not a publishing decision.

## What is licensed

The saved lead is the Department of Fish and Wildlife guide page. It defines a guide as a person who packs or guides, who for compensation assists another person in taking any bird, mammal, fish, amphibian, or reptile, or who for profit transports persons or their equipment to or from a hunting or fishing area. The license covers hunting and fishing. It is a person credential.

Fees on that page are valid February 1, 2027 through January 31, 2028. A resident guide license is $306.00. A nonresident guide license is $703.75. Guide employee registration is $67.21. An employee who guides only within that employment is exempt from the guide license when workers' compensation reporting, income-tax withholding reporting, and department employee registration are all in place before any contact with a person being guided. The page links application and renewal PDFs. It does not post a holder file. A License and Revenue Branch mailing address, a sales-office telephone, and an email are printed. Those were not copied.

The public guide lookup is a real search on the license-sales host. The permit-type list offers Guide. The search asks for a name, a permit number, and optional services, species, and counties. No bulk-export control was on the form. A blank statewide search was not run. Two Sonoma County queries were run on 2026-11-25:

- Sonoma County plus the service Fishing, General returned the page text "Showing 123 result(s)." All 123 cards listed Sonoma and Fishing, General. Sacramento was also listed on 107 of those 123 cards.
- Sonoma County with no service filter returned "Showing 161 result(s)." All 161 cards listed Sonoma. A Fishing service appeared on 137 of the 161. Fishing, General appeared on 123 of the 161.

The cards are person records. The visible columns are name, mailing address, phone number, services, species, and counties. Those contact fields were not copied, and the result pages were deleted after the counts were checked. A county on a card is a service area the record displays. It is not a departure port and not a boat. The 123 and the 161 are query results. They are not a Bodega Bay fleet and not a statewide guide census. Adding them would double-count people who list more than one county.

Commercial fishing items on the fee page are valid April 1, 2026 through March 31, 2027. Fees there include a nonrefundable 3 percent application fee, capped at $7.50 per item under Title 14, section 700.4. Online sales add a nonrefundable 5 percent license-agent handling fee. Four credentials on that page stay separate:

- A commercial fishing license is a person credential for taking fish for commercial purposes. Resident fee $188.75. Nonresident fee $554.75. Fish and Game Code section 70 defines resident. Section 86 defines take. This is not the guide license.
- Commercial boat registration is a vessel registration for an owner or operator of a vessel used in public waters in connection with fishing operations for profit, or which for profit permits persons to sport fish. Resident fee $487.80. Nonresident fee $1,448.25.
- A commercial passenger fishing vessel license is a boat credential required for any boat from which persons are allowed to sport fish for a fee. Fee $487.50.
- A commercial ocean enhancement stamp is $70.30. The page requires it for commercial passenger fishing vessels operating south of Point Arguello in Santa Barbara County, and for commercial fishers who take, possess, or land white seabass south of that point. Bodega Bay is north of that point. The stamp was not applied to this landing.

A commercial crab-trap validation is $18.80. The page requires it for a commercial passenger fishing vessel that fishes for crabs with crab traps. It is available beginning December 30, 2025, and required starting January 1, 2026. A Dungeness crab vessel permit on the same page is a separate commercial-take permit. Neither permit was sampled as a hull list.

The commercial passenger fishing vessel log site is an account for the license holder to submit fishing activity, including months with no fishing. Authority on the page is Fish and Game Code sections 7923 and 8026 and Title 14, sections 190 and 195. Paper logs remain the alternative. The site is not a public vessel roster. A help email is printed and was not copied.

The public-records guidelines say commercial fishing data, including landing receipts, are exempt except as provided in Fish and Game Code section 8022. Since March 1, 2021, requests go through the GovQA system. The guidelines name an office and a coordinator and publish telephone numbers. Those were not copied. Electronic records through GovQA have no fee. Photocopies are $0.15 a page.

## Bodega Bay landing

The HTTPS Regional Parks path for the Sport Fishing Center redirects to the county host that was opened. Sonoma County Regional Parks says it operates the Sport Fishing Center inside private Porto Bodega Marina, and that this is the place in Bodega Bay where privately chartered boats may dock to load and unload passengers. The page lists 18 private operators in alphabetical order, with a phone column. The phones were not copied. Sixteen labels say 6 passenger. One label says 20 passenger. One sailing label has no passenger count. Three labels use whale-watching or sailing in the name. The 18 is a landing list of booking names. It is not a documentation-number file and not a fishing-only census.

The same page says the docks run from 20 feet to 80 feet, with 7 berths, currently full, and a wait list. A transient dock is available through a dock-use agreement. The marina office telephone and email for that agreement were not copied. A 2026-2027 rates PDF is linked and was not opened. Seven berths are a facility count. They are not 18 hulls and not a countywide charter census.

## Reuse

A current page, a download, and authority to claim a listing are three different things.

| Publisher | Reuse status | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Department of Fish and Wildlife informational pages, including Conditions of Use | conditional | Conditions of Use, dated December 7, 2000, and subject to change without notice. Unmarked information on the site is described as public domain and may be copied as permitted by law. Photographs, unique branding, seals, and marks need permission. Information the State did not create needs the owner's permission. Government Code section 11015.5: electronically collected personal information about site users is not sold. The State may distribute certain lists and statistical reports of regulatory information as provided by law, and it says no personal information is sold or distributed. Public-records law controls if it conflicts with the policy. |
| Guide lookup on the license-sales host | not_granted | The application footer says Copyright 2026, State of California, All Rights Reserved. That footer is an "otherwise indicated" mark against the 2000 public-domain sentence. The result cards display name, mailing address, and phone. No export license is stated. |
| Commercial fee page, public-records guidelines, and the passenger-vessel log page | conditional for the department text; not_granted for log records | The fee page and the guidelines sit on the department site covered by Conditions of Use. The log site is an operator account. It does not offer another vessel's logs. The guidelines treat commercial fishing data, including landing receipts, as exempt except as Fish and Game Code section 8022 provides. |
| Sonoma County website, including the Sport Fishing Center page | conditional | County Terms of Use. Unmarked information on the county site is described as public domain and may be copied as permitted by law. Photographs and material the county did not create need the owner's permission. No warranty. The policy can change without notice. The Sport Fishing Center page itself has no separate copyright footer. The terms do not grant a commercial directory of the private operators named there. A webmaster address is published and was not copied. |
| Five operator publishers | not_granted (2), unknown (3) | Two booking pages carry a copyright line and no directory license. Three pages presented no terms URL. |

The highest-priority permission question is whether the 2000 public-domain sentence covers the 2026 guide-lookup application, and whether any guide, commercial-boat, or passenger-vessel file may be republished. The most useful next primary source is the guide-laws PDF linked from the guide page, then a public-records request for a vessel roster if the department maintains one. A county passenger label and an operator booking page do not authorize a commercial directory to claim a listing.

## Where to start

Read the guide license as a person credential and the commercial passenger fishing vessel license as a boat credential. Read commercial boat registration as its own vessel registration. Keep the commercial fishing license on the person who takes fish for sale. Until a public vessel file exists, a Bodega charter hull is an operator claim. Add a hull only when a dated public record prints a Coast Guard documentation number or a California boat or commercial registration number and that number matches one vessel.
