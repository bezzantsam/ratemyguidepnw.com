# Wisconsin fishing directory inventory

Checked 2026-11-25. Water in scope: Sheboygan charter departures. This note records the licensing lookups, the reuse terms that were actually printed, and aggregate sample counts. Individual boats, captains, and booking sites are not named here.

No one was contacted. No listing was published.

## What was found

Wisconsin uses two person credentials for paid fishing on the Great Lakes. Neither one is a Sheboygan boat list.

The guide license, Wis. Stat. 29.512, is required before a person is paid to guide, direct, or assist another person in hunting, fishing, or trapping. Hunting and trapping guide licenses are resident-only. No guide license may be issued under age 18. The Great Lakes guide page says this license covers fishing on the Great Lakes and tributaries other than sport trolling for salmon and trout, and that a U.S. Coast Guard license is still required to guide passengers from a vessel. A business that employs guides may itself hold a guide license and report for its staff. Licenses run January 1 through December 31 and are bought in Go Wild. The page's reporting locations for 2027 and onward are Green Bay for all species, Lake Michigan smallmouth bass in Door County waters only, and Lake Superior for all species. Sheboygan is not on that list. No guide roster is posted.

The outlying-water sport-trolling license is the credential for paid sport trolling for trout or salmon. Wis. Stat. 29.514 issues it to the person, not the boat. No license may be issued under age 18. The application must include the person's name and address, the home port, and a valid U.S. Coast Guard operator's license number. Boats used by the licensee must meet Coast Guard and Wisconsin boat requirements. The charter page says returning captains buy the license in Go Wild and new captains mail Form 9400-245. Licenses expire December 31. Electronic trip reports have been required since May 1, 2023. NR 20.60 requires each report to include a documented boat name or an 8-digit state registration number, the locations fished, anglers, hours, and harvest. Those reports are not posted.

Compiled s. 29.563(5) prints a resident guide fee of $39.25, a nonresident guide fee of $99.25, a resident sport-trolling fee of $100, and a nonresident Lake Michigan and Green Bay sport-trolling fee of $400. Form 9400-245, revision R 1/22, prints $100.25 and $400.25. A March 2022 DNR information sheet prints guide costs of $40 and $100 and the same $100.25 and $400.25 sport-trolling amounts. That sheet says it is an interpretation. Go Wild was not opened, so the amount charged today was not observed. The blank form's extracted text certifies that the Coast Guard number is current and does not show a home-port label. The statute still requires the home port.

The commercial-licenses page has no holder list. A credential-search host did not resolve on two attempts. No name was queried. The public-records page says email is the preferred request path, prints copying costs, and does not say whether a licensee list or the boat-name field would be released. No request was sent. The blank application says the department may provide application information to requesters under the public records law. That sentence is not a directory.

The Lake Michigan and Green Bay sportfishing tables estimate angler hours and trout and salmon harvest through 2025. In 2025 the charter column for the whole lake and Green Bay is 256,540 angler hours. Sheboygan County's 2025 total, all angler groups together, is 162,117 angler hours. Harvest tables also have a charter column of fish. None of those cells is a count of boats or of licensed departures.

## Sheboygan sample

Visit Sheboygan's fishing page names two public launches, including Harbor Centre Marina at 821 Broughton Drive, and does not name a charter boat. The city marina page returned HTTP 403. A second fetch returned a bot interstitial. A related marina host timed out twice. Search text about a charter fleet at the riverfront was not used.

Two operator booking pages were opened. One describes a single hull and also says several boats are available, so the business is not one hull. A marketplace listing for that business returned HTTP 403. A search extract used different sentences for the same length and builder and was not treated as an independent measurement. The other operator repeats one length and builder on its own trip cards. No second publisher was downloaded. Neither opened page showed a documentation or registration number. Both hulls stay held.

The two holds are not added to the creel hours or to either license program. The licensed Sheboygan departure population is null.

## Reuse

DNR acceptable use says website material is for the noncommercial use of the general public and that fair-use guidelines apply. The department remains the owner of specifically copyrighted information it created and posted. Use outside fair use requires permission from the page contact. If reproduction is approved, the credit must read: Reproduced with permission from the Department of Natural Resources. Links to dnr.wi.gov are welcome. A link is not an endorsement. No permission was requested.

That policy covers the DNR pages and PDFs opened here. It does not state terms for a public-records file that has not been released. Operator pages are outside it. The retrieved operator text states no reuse license. The tourism page states none either.

## Unresolved

The open question is whether a current sport-trolling list, home-port list, or boat-name file would be released, and on what terms. The records page is the request path. It was not used.

Also unresolved: which dollar amount Go Wild charges, because the statute, the R 1/22 form, and the March 2022 sheet disagree, and whether the live application still collects the home port the statute requires.

The most useful next primary source that can be read without a request is the open-water effort narrative linked from the Lake Michigan management reports page. The creel tables do not separate Sheboygan charter hours from the county total. The Lake Michigan grid map and the blank monthly report form were linked and not opened.
