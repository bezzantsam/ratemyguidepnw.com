# Ohio Lake Erie guide sources

Checked 2026-11-25. Scope is the licensed fishing-guide program for the Lake Erie fishing district, and a Port Clinton and Catawba Island departure check. This note records methodology, aggregate counts, primary-source links, and unresolved permission questions. It does not identify operators or people, and it does not publish a directory.

## Credential

Ohio Revised Code 1533.51 requires a license from the chief of the Division of Wildlife before a person serves as a fishing guide in the Lake Erie fishing district. The license is issued to a person, costs $50, and expires each April 15. It is carried by the person in command or in charge. Ohio Revised Code 1531.01 defines a fishing guide as a person who, for consideration or hire, operates a boat or furnishes angling devices, ice shanties, or other fishing equipment, and accompanies or assists another person to fish.

Ohio Administrative Code 1501:31-3-11 requires a United States Coast Guard merchant mariner credential when the guide takes clients on a vessel for hire where that credential is required. The same rule requires a Division of Wildlife fishing-guide decal on a boat operated for consideration as a fishing-guide boat. The decal is serialized, annual, and issued to a person. Displaying another person's decal is unlawful unless the guiding person's own decal is also displayed. The rule also makes it unlawful to copy the guide license document itself. That prohibition is about the credential the guide carries. It is not a stated rule for the public opt-in roster.

The public roster is not a vessel roll. The paper application, Division form DNR 8950 (revision 0224), collects a boat name, registration or federal documentation number, and length, plus docking places. Those fields are not in the public file. A business name on the public file can be shared by more than one licensed person, so the business name is not a hull.

## Waters

Ohio Revised Code 1533.02 defines the Lake Erie fishing district as the waters of Lake Erie, a described portion of Sandusky Bay, and a described portion of Maumee Bay. The same section places East Harbor, West Harbor, and Middle Harbor, in Ottawa County, in the inland fishing district. Section 1533.51 does not create a separate inland-guide license. Kayak, shore, and ice categories found in this pass sit inside the Lake Erie fishing-district guide license.

Catawba Landing is on West Harbor. A departure from that harbor is not, by itself, proof that the trip is inside the licensed district. The license still applies when the person serves as a fishing guide in the Lake Erie fishing district.

## Departure sample

Catawba Landing's own marina pages, checked the same day, describe dockage on West Harbor and do not publish a charter roster.

One still-online page, last modified 2022-01-14, presents five featured guide names at Catawba Landing Marina in Port Clinton. That page is the audited departure roster. It is not a slip census, and it is not the marina's current site. A longer 2022 West Harbor featured index was opened and was not row-audited.

All five featured names were compared with a current first-party site and with the 2026-09-04 opt-in PDF. One row was supported. Four were held. One independent publisher confirmed the supported row.

The supported publisher states a dock at Catawba Landing and names two hulls, with length and make, and no registration or documentation number. Two opt-in rows use that business. The license records do not assign either person to either hull. The 2022 featured page already described those two hulls; the publisher's current boats page still names both.

The four holds were: a linked hostname that did not resolve; a linked hostname that did not serve a charter site; a linked site with no booking content; and a current site that places the operation at a different marina, outside this Catawba sample, with no matching business name on the opt-in PDF.

Additional sample: 2 rows, 1 supported, 1 held, 2 independent publisher matches. Arithmetic: 2 = 1 + 1.

These two sites were chosen because they are current and bookable. They are not a random sample of the 576 opt-in rows, and they are not a count of Port Clinton guides.

## Commercial reuse

A current public list, a right to copy it into a commercial directory, and authority to claim a listing are separate.

- The Ohio Laws disclaimer (https://codes.ohio.gov/legal/disclaimer) is a liability waiver. It does not grant commercial reuse of the Revised Code or Administrative Code.
- ODNR's privacy policy (https://ohiodnr.gov/odnr-help-center/privacy-notices-and-policies) says the Department does not sell, rent, or lease its customer lists to third parties. It does not say whether a third party may copy the already posted opt-in PDF into a commercial directory.
- ODNR's public-records page (https://ohiodnr.gov/discover-and-learn/safety-conservation/about-ODNR/policies/public-records) describes inspection and copies under Ohio Revised Code 149.43, a $0.05 per-page copy fee, no fee for viewing or email, and redaction under Chapters 149 and 1347. That is an access process. It is not a commercial-directory license. Requests can go through the Public Records Center linked from that page.
- The application checkbox authorizes the Division to list the applicant on the Division's website and publications. It does not mention third-party directories.
- Operator, marina, Sea Grant, and association pages checked here do not state a commercial-reuse grant. Silence is unknown.

No bulk feed other than the single PDF was found. Attribution requirements for republication were not stated. Nothing checked here authorizes this directory to claim a listing for a guide.
