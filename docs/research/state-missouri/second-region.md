## License check

The fishing-permit catalog was opened again on 2026-11-25. It remains an angler list. Daily fishing is $9 for residents and nonresidents. The annual fishing permit is $14 for a resident and $57 for a nonresident. The trout permit is $12 for a resident, $24 for a nonresident, and $6 for youth ages 0–15. The trout-permit page repeats those prices. It requires the trout permit to possess trout, except in trout parks where a daily trout fishing tag is required, and it requires the trout permit for all fishing year-round in Lake Taneycomo upstream from the U.S. Highway 65 bridge. A fishing permit, or an exemption, is still required. The catalog publishes no guide roster. The sport-guide count remains unknown.

The Lake Taneycomo special-waterbody page is a creel page for Taney County. On the lake and its tributaries, brown trout have a 20-inch minimum, and the daily limit is 4 trout combined with only 1 brown trout. No fishing is allowed within 760 feet below Table Rock Dam. From that closed zone to the mouth of Fall Creek, rainbow trout have a 12- to 20-inch protected slot, only pole-and-line fishing is allowed, and only flies and artificial lures may be used. Porous-soled waders are prohibited. From the closed zone to the U.S. Highway 65 bridge, a fishing permit, unless the angler is exempt, and a trout permit are required to fish for any species. The page says the lake is stocked monthly with rainbow trout and annually with brown trout. It publishes no guide list.

Sampled equals supported plus held on every row. The sample total is 4 = 1 supported + 3 held. No second publisher was opened. A landing is supported from its own pages. A guide product with one publisher and no hull is held.

Scotty's Trout Dock is supported as a landing. The home page offers boat rentals and kayak rentals on Lake Taneycomo. The rental page lists a 24-foot pontoon class seating up to 12, with a choice of a 60 hp or a 115 hp motor, at $285 for a half day and $385 for a full day, tax and fuel extra. A 16.5-foot three-seat V-bottom bass boat is $185 and $280. A 17.5-foot four-seat bass boat is $189 and $289. The same page also prices rate-sheet labels, including a 20-foot and 24-foot pair, a 115 hp boat, a Ranger or Briarwood line, and a ski boat. Those labels were not matched to a hull name, HIN, or official number. The footer copyright is 2026.

The dock's guide page is held. It says the dock refers guides. The guides supply bait, tackle, equipment, the boat, and fuel, and they clean and bag kept fish. Pontoon guide trips are for 4 to 6 people. Trips typically leave at 8 a.m. and return around noon. Four-hour prices are $350 for 1 or 2 people, $425 for 3, and $500/$600 for 4 to 6. Six-hour prices are $475, $525, and $550/$750 on those same party sizes. The page requires a fishing license and a trout permit, and a youth trout permit under 16. It names no guide and no hull. A boat-rental notice says a person born after January 1, 1984, needs a boater license to drive a boat, and it attributes that sentence to the Missouri State Water Patrol. That notice is a rental rule. It was not checked against the statute.

CastAway Charters is held. The site sells Lake Taneycomo trips: a 4-hour trip for 1 person starting at $325, a 4-hour trip for 2 to 4 people starting at $375, a 6-hour trip with the price given only by telephone, and a 4-hour trophy night trip starting at $450. Spin and fly fishing are both offered. The about text says the service has guided this lake for more than 20 years. The closing paragraph says trips include gear and Coast Guard-approved safety equipment. No hull name, length, HIN, or official number is on the page. A video credit names Lilleys' Landing. That credit was not used as a cross-check, and the resort site was not opened. Paths `/privacy` and `/privacy-policy` returned HTTP 404.

Table Rock Guide Service is held for its Taneycomo product. The page also uses the name Branson Guided Fishing Trips for guides it says are employed or referred. Those are one publisher. Duration prices for 1 or 2 people, then 3 people, then $50 for each person over 3 up to 6, are $250 and $300 for 2 hours, $300 and $350 for 3 hours, $375 and $425 for 4 hours, and $495 and $545 for 6 hours. A pontoon is $695 for 4 hours and $825 for 6 hours, up to 6 people. A $100 deposit holds the date. Credit cards add 4 percent. Cancellation at 7 days or more avoids a fee. Inside 7 days the fee is $100, refunded if the guide rebooks. Inside 48 hours, and for a no-show, the full trip amount is due. The boat block describes a 2024 Skeeter SX241 center console with a Yamaha 300. That is a class and a model year. The page says the Coast Guard limits the vessel to 6 passengers and that the service can field up to 12 licensed guides for groups. The 12 is a capacity claim. No guide was named, and none was sampled. The meeting place is arranged the day before, so the page does not name a departure dock. The page prints an adult trout price of $12 for both residents and nonresidents. The MDC trout-permit page prints $12 for a resident and $24 for a nonresident. The department page is the credential price. The same operator page prints the $9 daily fishing permit and the $6 youth trout permit, which match the catalog. Paths `/privacy-policy/` and `/terms/` returned HTTP 404. The page carries a copyright line for Table Rock Fishing Guide.

No unique hull was identified.
