Checked 2026-11-25. Scope is the North Platte near Casper: guide credentials, the public registration path, boat-number access, and one department access site in the Casper metro area. This note records what each publisher states about reuse and the aggregate result of one defined sample. It does not name people, businesses, license numbers, or client records. Private evidence stays outside this repository.

No one was contacted. Nothing here is a listing, a claim, or a publishing decision.

## Four records, kept apart

A paid fishing trip on the North Platte can touch four different records. A booking page does not establish any of them.

**Angler fishing license.** The department sells fishing licenses through its electronic licensing system. That purchase path is separate from the commercial guided-fishing boat sticker. No angler identifier was submitted.

**Big-game outfitter or professional guide.** Codified Law 23-2-407, read from the legislature's title 23 PDF, requires a license before a person holds himself or herself out as an outfitter, or provides guide or packing services, for the purpose of taking any big or trophy game animal. That sentence is a hunting credential. It is not a fishing-guide license.

**Commercially guided fishing boat.** The saved lead is a purchase page for a commercial guided-fishing boat and trailer sticker. A current sticker is required before launching on a commercially guided fishing trip. The fee on that page is $325, with replacement fees of $25 for one sticker and $50 for the boat-and-trailer pair. Purchase requires a Game and Fish user account. No account was used. Codified Law 23-3-206, effective for this registration beginning January 1, 2026, defines a commercially guided fishing boat as a fishing boat operated by an outfitter or professional fishing guide, for financial gain, to accompany and assist a person on a river or lake for fishing. Commission Chapter 71, signed September 10, 2025, and effective January 1, 2026, carries that program out. The registrant is the person who purchased the sticker. The boat sticker shows "WYO", "GUIDE", the calendar year, and an identification number. The trailer sticker does not carry that number. Stickers may be transferred between watercraft, and a permanently affixed sticker ends when the boat or trailer is transferred to a new owner. A sticker number is therefore not a hull name and not a hull identification number. Trip records go to the department twice a year. The online reporting address redirects to a login. No login was submitted. No public registrant roster was found.

**The water, the landing, and the hull.** North Platte River-Mills Bridge is a department public-access area in the town of Mills, inside the Casper metro area, with parking and a primitive boat ramp. The commission holds an easement. The page says the site is open year round, a fire ban was in effect on the check date, and hunting, trapping, camping, and fires are prohibited. Motorboat numbering is a separate department certificate under Codified Law 41-13-102 and 41-13-104. The certificate states the number and the owner's name and address. Subsection 41-13-104(e) says the department's records under that section are public records. The online watercraft page is an owner or co-owner login for a one-year or three-year registration. No account was used, and no public hull search was found.

## Reuse

The department privacy policy says use of state web pages is governed by Wyoming law and that information collected there may be subject to the public-records statutes, beginning at Codified Law 16-4-201. The policy does not grant commercial-directory reuse, it does not state an attribution line, and it does not describe a bulk feed. The department footer asserts copyright 2026 and all rights reserved.

Codified Law 16-4-202(a), read from the current title 16 PDF, says all public records are open for inspection by any person at reasonable times during the entity's business hours, except as the act or another law provides. Section 16-4-203(a) requires inspection unless it would violate a state statute, a federal statute or regulation, or another ground in the subsections that follow. Those later subsections were not finished. Section 16-4-204 allows copies for a reasonable fee set by the custodian. Inspection and a copy fee are not a commercial-directory grant.

Codified Law 23-1-706 says personally identifiable information submitted for a game and fish license, permit, or tag is not a public record unless the applicant designates it as public. The listed fields include electronic mail, date of birth, telephone number, driver's license number, Social Security number, passport number, sportsperson identification number, and tax identification number. The sticker purchase page repeats that withholding and says other application information, including name and address, may be public. No application was filed, so no row was tested.

The department's public-records page says a request should identify the records and the date range, and that production costs above $180 are charged after a $180 credit. The page prints a department inbox. No request was sent.

The electronic-licensing privacy notice says information provided may be subject to the Public Records Act, and that a Social Security number is not disclosed except to the Department of Family Services for child support. The next clause, about date of birth and financial account numbers, is incomplete as rendered. The notice points to the Attorney General for additional privacy text. That page was not opened.

One rendered operator site asserts all rights reserved for 2026. Another carries a 2025 copyright line and does not add a reuse sentence. The third rendered operator site is silent. Silence is unknown. No marketing copy is repeated here.

No source checked on this pass grants bulk commercial reuse of a named guide list. No list exists in the sources that were read, and none was downloaded.

## Unresolved

The permission question is whether inspection and copying under Codified Law 16-4-202 and 16-4-204, read against the department's copyright claim, authorize commercial-directory reuse of access-site facts or of any boat-sticker or watercraft-certificate record that is a public record. The most useful next primary source is the Attorney General privacy page linked from the licensing notice. It was not opened. The unread remainder of section 16-4-203 is the companion text.

Also unresolved: whether any later codified section creates a person-level fishing-guide license. Which North Platte ramp, if any, is the departure for the held bookings. Mills Bridge was not named on those pages. Whether a motorboat certificate can be obtained as a public record with a hull identification number and without a home address. Whether the general regulations linked from the Mills Bridge page limit commercial guiding on that access site.
