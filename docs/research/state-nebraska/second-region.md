# Nebraska Lewis and Clark Lake coverage

Checked 2026-11-25. Phase `nebraska-second-region`. The water in this pass is Lewis and Clark Lake, the Missouri River reservoir behind Gavins Point Dam, and only guides based in Nebraska. Lake McConaughy and the statewide credential overview stay in the inventory note. This file is the incremental public method. Business names, guide names, hull nicknames, phone numbers, email addresses, and street addresses stay outside this repository.

No one was contacted. This file is not a listing and not a publishing decision.

## What this pass checked

The saved lead, special occasion permits, was read again. It is still written permission for an event. The application is due at least 30 days ahead. Fishing tournaments must also be registered on a separate tournament page. That tournament page was linked and not opened. The permit page has no Lewis and Clark holder field.

The Lewis and Clark State Recreation Area page has no guide roster. It describes the Nebraska shore park, lists fish species, and says the marina store is open. A banner says Weigand Marina boat slips and the boat ramp are closed beginning Sept. 11, 2025, through 2026. The same page states 116 leased seasonal dock slips, 10 daily rental slips, and four ramps in the Weigand area. Those figures describe the facility. They are not a count of boats in the water during the closure.

The Weigand Marina FAQ is a 2027 expansion design. It says the marina is welcoming boaters for the 2027 season. Slipholder surveys are due Dec. 31, 2026. The acceptance and deposit window is Jan. 14-21, 2027. Remaining slips are to be offered to the public by Feb. 17, 2027. The opened page sets the deposit at $500, applied to the slip. Planned boat slips are 30 of 26-foot uncovered inner, 36 of 26-foot covered inner, 68 of 32-foot covered, 82 of 32-foot uncovered outer, 6 of 32-foot uncovered end caps, 34 of 40-foot covered outer, 6 of 40-foot uncovered outer, and 2 of 40-foot uncovered end caps. Those boat slips sum to 264. The plan adds 24 uncovered personal-watercraft slips. The labeled total is 288. Clearances on the page are dock and waterline dimensions, not measured hulls.

A Nov. 3, 2026 news release asks for interest in future boat or personal-watercraft rentals at Weigand Marina, with a Dec. 1, 2026 deadline. It is not an awarded-vendor list and not a guide census.

The September 2026 river-outfitter PDF was keyword-checked and not recounted. The first page prints "Updated July 2026." The inventory note that recorded no revision date on that first page is left as written. This pass found no Lewis, Clark, Crofton, Yankton, Gavins, or Weigand string. Missouri and Niobrara hits in that file are paddling text.

A Nebraskaland Magazine story on walleye movement includes a caption that associates one fishing guide of Yutan, Nebraska, with a fish caught on this lake, and the body says area fishing guides helped a tagging study. The caption gives no booking page, hull, or departure. That person was not sampled. Names were not copied.

## Where to start

Use the recreation-area page for access status and the Weigand FAQ for the 2027 slip plan. Neither page lists a guide. A Nebraska-based guide for this lake still needs a first-party page that states a Nebraska base and, before any hull is added, a registration or documentation number. A South Dakota meet point does not become a Nebraska base because the marketing line says Nebraska.
