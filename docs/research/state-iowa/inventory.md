# Iowa fishing-guide inventory

Checked 2026-11-25. Scope is bookable fishing guides on the Iowa Great Lakes, the Dickinson County chain. This note is methodology, aggregate counts, primary-source links, and unresolved permission questions. It does not identify operators or people.

## Credential

The assigned lead is the Department of Natural Resources fishing regulations page. That page describes a sport fishing license. Residents and nonresidents 16 and older need a valid fishing license while fishing. The fee page lists a resident fishing license at $22.00 and a nonresident fishing license at $48.00, plus shorter-term, trout, and boundary-water products: ten resident rows and seven nonresident rows in those two tables. The same regulations page says a sport fishing license does not authorize buying or selling fish, mussels, turtles, frogs, or bait. A bait dealer's license is a separate credential for taking bait for commercial purposes. The page points to Iowa Code 481A.142, which was not opened.

A person supervising a group fishing outing must have an individual fishing license to fish with the group. The eRegulations license page states that change and dates the page September 23, 2026. That requirement is still an angler license.

The 2026 Iowa Code chapter 483A section index is the fishing and hunting license chapter. No title on that index is a fishing-guide, charter, or outfitter license. Section 483A.28 is titled noncommercial harvest of aquatic species. Section 483A.22A, in the PDF last modified December 13, 2025, says the department may set fees for lists of licensees, and that the fee may exceed the cost of preparing the list and providing the copying service, notwithstanding section 22.3. The section does not publish a list. Section 22.3 was not opened. No rule under 483A.22A was opened.

The Go Outdoors Iowa customer-lookup page is an account login. One control lists the account types Charter Captain, Pier, Vessel, and Charter Boat, then asks for a customer identifier, a pier name, a certificate number, and a PIN. The form was not submitted. The page does not publish a roster or a count. The same HTML contains a MySD single-sign-on string, so those four labels were not treated as proof that Iowa issues a fishing-guide license.

Chapter 482, section 4, is a commercial gear license. The PDF last modified December 13, 2025, says a person shall not use commercial gear unless someone at the site has the appropriate valid commercial license, valid from the date of issue through January 10 of the next calendar year. It states a resident commercial fisher fee of $200 and a nonresident fee of $400. The Department commercial-fishing page states $202 and $402 for those two rows and lists fourteen fee rows in all, covering fishers, helpers, roe, and turtles. The two dollar figures were not reconciled. The Department page says commercial fishers take fish from the Mississippi and Missouri rivers. That page also describes aquaculture units and bait dealers. None of those rows is an Iowa Great Lakes sport-guide roster. The issued guide count is unknown.

The 2026 chapter 462A index is water navigation. Its titles include boat liveries and an engineer or pilot license. Those section texts were not opened, so they were not used as a fishing-guide credential. The 40.88 MB regulations booklet linked from the assigned page was not downloaded. An older administrative-code link, chapter 571.77, was not opened.

Fishing tournaments on public waters need a Department special-event permit. That permit is not a guide license.

## Association list

No Department roster of fishing guides was found. The open association page is the Iowa Great Lakes Fishing Club guides page. It has 18 headings. Two are fish-cleaning businesses. The other 16 are guide, charter, or hardwater-trip cards. The page says a local guide is listed by becoming a member. The footer copyright is 2024, all rights reserved. No card states a length, a make, a hull name, or a registration number. Absence from this member list is not a finding that someone is unlicensed.

Four of the 16 trip cards were compared with a current first-party page that rendered text. None was supported. Four were held. Sampled = supported + held: 4 = 0 + 4.

One held row offers trips and describes a comfortable boat. Neither the operator page nor either directory states a length, a make, or a hull name. The stripped operator page has no booking control.

One held row has a length and a make on the operator page, a booking control, and a stated capacity of the party plus the operator. The association card names the business and does not state the length or the make. The operator page and the association card do not use the same business string. A shared website and a shared person are not a second measurement. No hull name or registration number was on the pages read. Seating capacity is not a second hull.

One held row has a booking control. The association card and the operator page name the business. Neither states a length, a make, or a registration number. The card also names waters outside the Iowa Great Lakes chain. The operator page says northern Iowa and southern Minnesota.

One held row has a booking control. The association card and the tourism directory name the business. The operator page names no boat length, make, or hull. It mentions portable ice-fishing shelters and does not give them a measurement.

Eight trip cards were not opened. Three operator hosts failed and were left outside the four: one failed three times, and two did not resolve. One host returned a page whose visible text was a short script shell, including a copyright year of 2035, and was not treated as a boat page.

## Tourism directory

Vacation Okoboji's fishing directory is a second publisher. It prints 16 results, 12 on the first page and 4 on the second. Seven titles use Guide or Charters. Two use Expeditions. Seven are a shop, a rental, a hatchery, or another title that does not say guide. The footer copyright is 2025, all rights reserved. No result states a hull measurement. This directory was not sampled as its own set of observations. Two of the four held rows also appear here as business names. That overlap was not counted as two hulls or as a second sample. 0 = 0 + 0 for this directory.

## Commercial reuse

A license requirement, a public list, and authority to copy either one into a commercial directory are separate. No public guide list was found.

- The Iowa.gov policies page, last modified November 23, 2026, uses its copyright section for infringement notices. It does not grant republication of agency pages. Its public-disclosure section is about information collected from visitors and names Iowa Code chapter 22. Chapter 22 was not opened. Silence on copying a licensee list is unknown.
- The Legislature disclaimer says website information is provided as is, without warranty, and distinguishes official PDF editions. The footer carries a copyright mark. The reproduction limits on that page are about photographs. Silence on the code text is unknown.
- Section 483A.22A is fee authority for lists of licensees. It is not a grant to republish names in a directory.
- The eRegulations license page reserves all rights. A linked reprint is not a Department copyright grant.
- The fishing-club page and both Vacation Okoboji fishing pages reserve all rights. The privacy page does not add a directory grant.
- Of the operator pages that rendered, one reserves all rights. The others say nothing about reuse. Silence is unknown. A booking control on an operator site is not a third-party directory grant.

Nothing checked here authorizes this directory to claim a listing.

## Unresolved

The permission question to answer first: with no public guide roster, do Iowa Code section 22.3 or a rule under section 483A.22A let a third party buy a licensee list and copy it into a commercial directory? The statute that was read allows a fee above copying cost. It does not name guides, and it does not grant republication.

Also unresolved: whether the navigation chapter's pilot license or boat-livery section is a charter credential; why the commercial fisher fee is $200 in the December 13, 2025 code PDF and $202 on the Department page; and which of the unopened association cards, if any, names a hull that a second publisher also measures.
